Junior AI/ML Engineer

About the Role:
We are looking for a passionate Junior AI/ML Engineer to join our growing research and development team. You will work on designing, training, and deploying machine learning models and building pipelines.

Responsibilities:
- Collaborate with software engineers to integrate ML models into production services.
- Clean, preprocess, and analyze raw datasets for model training.
- Assist in designing and evaluating Deep Learning algorithms for natural language processing.
- Maintain and monitor models in production using Docker containers.

Requirements:
- Bachelor or B.Tech degree in Computer Science, engineering, or related field.
- Minimum 1 year of professional experience in software development or data science.
- Strong proficiency in Python programming.
- Hands-on experience with Machine Learning libraries including PyTorch and scikit-learn.
- Familiarity with SQL and relational databases like PostgreSQL.
- Git for version control and collaborative development.

Preferred Qualifications:
- M.Tech or PhD in AI/ML is a plus.
- Familiarity with NLP, LLM, and RAG architectures.
- Experience deploying containers in Kubernetes.
- Knowledge of cloud services like AWS or GCP.
